# Surface brief: index.html (landing page)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor on a phone, arriving from Google Maps or Instagram, wants price-today, open-now, route, and a way to book. Primary action: reservar (WhatsApp → link → tel fallback); secondary: ligar, rota.

Proof on hand: Google 4,3★ / 2.676 avaliações (03/10/2026), real prices, real hours, paraphrased review themes. No testimonials, no photos yet (placeholders).

Build path: code-led (no image generation available). Brief pins palette, fonts, card language and section order; the roll governs composition and the signature interaction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the restaurant's painted price board, and it knows what time it is in Teresina. It refuses the category default of a dark steak-photo hero with centered slogan and a generic menu grid.

OWN-WORLD: Northeastern hand-painted signage (abridor de letras) on a limewashed wall (parede de cal). Every section ground is a step of warm cream; vinho survives as ink (slab headings, body) and as object — the price cards, the dish cards, the hero HOJE chip, the hero photo mat and the closing footer band. Dense reading sits on a lighter creme placa raised off the wall by the filete and a short sepia shadow. Orange only for actions and the HOJE marker; the filete is laranja on vinho and laranja-tinta on cal; the brush word is laranja-tinta, unshaded. Alfa Slab One caps, Kaushan brush word rotated a few degrees overlapping the slab line, Nunito body. Every board has a rounded corner and a thin orange filete with an inner amber hairline. Prices painted like a quadro: big integer, small raised cents, small R$.

DIRECTION CHANGE (06/10/2026): the original Parede Rule (the wall is always vinho) was replaced on the owner's instruction — the wall is cal, vinho is figure. Everything else in this contract holds.

STORY: Know price-today and open-now in the first viewport; trust it from the Google seal; see the full board; see the food and room; find the way; book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390px: status strip (dot + "Aberto agora · fecha às 23:00"), header (logo, Reservar, menu). Headline in slab caps with brush "família toda", subtitle, a HOJE chip reading today's current price from the price board, primary Reservar mesa (orange) + Ver valores, Google seal. Hero photo framed as a sign board below on mobile, right column (4:5) on desktop.

FORM: Letreiro pintado / quadro de preços, candidate 3 of 7, seed 79762154. Raises: (Hardware bench + Film) one orange action per viewport, orange never decorative. (Drum machine) one running "now" marker lights today across hero chip, price board and hours table. (Depot blind) the status strip is one fixed window; state changes step a whole line. (Comic) brush lettering reserved for three moments: hero, infantil, final CTA. Declined: Versailles (kept nothing).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
